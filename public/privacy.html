Privacy Policy

01. Core Architecture: Local-First Shield

Most modern productivity and alarm applications monetize user behavior by uploading sleep cycles, voice recordings, and activity timestamps to remote cloud infrastructures. Morro rejects this business model entirely.

All core alarm execution mechanics, routine schedules, and operational parameters execute locally within the operating system sandbox of your Android device. Your smartphone is the sovereign host; we maintain no remote database containing your morning habits or personal recordings.

02. Audio Capture & the RECORD_AUDIO Permission

To power the Voice Commitment feature, Morro requests runtime access to your microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO).

Strict Storage Mandate: Audio recordings are captured in 16000Hz mono AAC format and written directly into your private, isolated application storage sandbox (context.filesDir).

Voice recordings are never transmitted to cloud servers, never parsed through third-party speech-to-text APIs, and never made accessible to other applications installed on your operating system.

When you record tomorrow's commitment or preview past commitments, playback occurs exclusively via native local Android media streams (STREAM_ALARM). If you delete an alarm or uninstall Morro, your audio commitments are permanently purged from your device's filesystem registers.

03. The Isolated Subscription & Billing Exception

Morro is funded directly by its users via subscriptions rather than surveillance advertising or corporate data brokerage. Consequently, network connectivity is activated for only one isolated operation:

  • Entitlement & Billing: Communicating with Google Play Billing to verify your active subscription status and confirm valid licensing credentials.

No operational alarm data, audio memos, or personal task descriptions are appended to this validation handshake. If network connectivity is unavailable (e.g., Airplane Mode, dead cellular towers, or off-grid travel), cached license tokens ensure your scheduled alarms continue to execute reliably without delay.

04. Website Cookies & Web Analytics Policy

Our public website (morrolandingpage.marhaanansari.workers.dev) is designed with the same privacy discipline as the native application:

  • Zero Cross-Site Tracking: We do not load Facebook Pixels, Google Tag Manager cross-site advertising scripts, or invasive fingerprinting beacons.
  • No Persistent Advertising Cookies: We do not deploy cookies to build profiling dossiers or track your browsing activity across other properties.
  • Volatile Context Only: The landing page extracts transient URL referral parameters (such as ?ref=) in temporary memory strictly to attribute beta waitlist reservations accurately.

05. The Beta Waitlist

When you submit your address, we collect one thing: the email address you typed. We do not ask for your name, and we build no profile around you. The form asks you to confirm you have read this notice before the field unlocks, and it asks separately, with an unticked box, whether you also want launch emails.

Signing up does not put you on the list. Your address is stored as pending and receives one message only: a link that asks you to confirm. Nothing else is sent unless you press that button, so nobody can add an address they do not own and nobody can make us mail a stranger. Until you confirm, your address is not counted, not mailed, and not visible to anyone. Confirmation links work once, expire after 48 hours, and are stored only as one-way hashes, so even a database leak cannot be turned into a working link.

Why we hold it. To send your closed-beta invite and the few setup messages needed to get you installed. That is a pre-contract step taken at your request, and it rests on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Launch marketing is a separate, optional choice: if you leave the launch-updates box unticked, we will only contact you about beta access.

Who processes it. Cloudflare (Workers and its D1 database) stores the record and runs the signup endpoint; Resend (resend.com) delivers the confirmation email. Both act as our processors. We do not sell addresses, do not share them for advertising, and load no ad pixels, tracking beacons or analytics scripts on this site.

How long we keep it. An address that never confirms is deleted 30 days after the last confirmation email. A confirmed address is kept until the beta closes and your invite has been sent, or until you ask us to delete it, and in any case no longer than 24 months after you sign up. Unsubscribing deletes the address rather than flagging it.

What we record. Alongside the address we store the date you accepted this notice and the version of the notice you accepted (currently 2026-11-17), so we can show what was agreed to. If this notice changes materially, we ask again before relying on the older acceptance.

Every marketing email carries a working unsubscribe link and our postal identity, and we act on an unsubscribe within 10 business days. You can also withdraw consent or ask for deletion at any time by writing to marhaanansari@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

The list is never public. No endpoint, counter, dashboard or field on this site reports how many people have joined. The figure shown near the form is a fixed statement, not a live query, so nobody can measure, scrape or infer the size of the list, and no visitor can see who else is on it.

06. Your Rights Over the Waitlist Record

You can ask us for a copy of the address we hold, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to our using it. Write to marhaanansari@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will confirm within 30 days. Deleting your waitlist record costs you nothing and does not affect any beta place you have already been given.

If we ever send marketing email, you may also complain to your local data-protection authority. In the United Kingdom that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the EU, your national supervisory authority; in India, the Data Protection Board of India. We would rather you told us first, so we can fix it.

07. Data Erasure & User Control

Because Morro maintains no remote profile of your voice or daily routine, you possess total, instantaneous control over your data. Clearing app storage in Android Settings or uninstalling Morro permanently destroys all voice commitments, logs, and local configuration files.
